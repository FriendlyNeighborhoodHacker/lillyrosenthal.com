Lilly Rosenthal is a senior at Hackley School in Tarrytown, New York, where she has spent four years on stage in everything from musical comedy to Greek-myth adventure. She most recently played Annabeth in *The Lightning Thief: The Percy Jackson Musical*, a role that let her combine sharp comic timing with a belt that reached the back row of the Allen Theater.

Her other favorite roles include Gomez in *The Addams Family*, where she led the company through "When You're an Addams" in a pencil mustache and a very large smile, and Snoopy in *You're a Good Man, Charlie Brown*, whose "Suppertime" became the number audiences hummed on the way to the parking lot.

Offstage, Lilly is an officer of Hackley's student government and a competitive public speaker. She has delivered keynote and campaign speeches to audiences of several hundred, and she credits the debate podium with teaching her how to hold a room without a script.

She trains in voice and musical theater, studies scene work and improvisation in the summer, and is happiest in a rehearsal room with a cold brew and a script covered in pencil notes. She plans to continue studying acting and theater in college.

Lilly lives in Scarsdale, New York with her parents, her brother Charlie, her sister Eve, and a dog who has never once hit his mark.
