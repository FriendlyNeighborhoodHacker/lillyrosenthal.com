Lilly Rosenthal is a senior at Hackley School in Tarrytown, New York where has had lead roles in You’re a Good Man, Charlie Brown (Snoopy, 2024), Percy Jackson (Annabeth, 2025), and The Addams Family (Gomez, 2026).

Lilly founded Hackley’s Improv Comedy club and has studied sketch comedy, scene work, and improv over the summers with Second City in Chicago and Brooklyn. She made her comedy debut winning her middle school’s speech contest doing stand-up comedy. 

Offstage, Lilly is the Vice President of Hackley’s Student Body, where she loves to bring drama into making schoolwide announcements and community-wide presentations fun.  In addition, Lilly is one of the co-captains of the Hackley Debate Team. She also started “Hackley Tech Angels,” a group of high school students that build software for the community.

Lilly is looking forward to continuing to perform in musical theater in college.
