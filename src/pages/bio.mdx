Lilly Rosenthal is a senior at Hackley School in Tarrytown, New York where has had lead roles in You're a Good Man, Charlie Brown (Snoopy, 2024), Percy Jackson (Annabeth, 2025), and The Addams Family (Gomez, 2026).

Offstage, Lilly is the Vice President of Hackley's Student Body.  She's one of the co-captains of the Hackley Debate Team.  She started the Hackley Improv club and also "Hackley Tech Angels," a group of high school students that build software for the community.
